About

I am a DPhil student in the University of Oxford’s Department of Materials, supervised by Dr Enzo Liotti and Professor Patrick Grant.

My research examines hot tearing in recycled aluminium alloys using real-time, in-situ X-ray imaging. By observing interdendritic flow during solidification and applying computer-vision methods to experimental data, I aim to clarify how defects nucleate and propagate in alloys produced from recycled sources.

Before Oxford, I studied Materials Engineering and Nanotechnology at Politecnico di Milano and Materials Science and Engineering at University College Dublin. I then worked as a Materials Applications Engineer at GE Aerospace, developing advanced manufacturing and repair processes for metallic components.

Project and poster award · 2026 Investigating interdendritic flow during aluminium alloy solidification Read the full story +

Hot tears form in the semi-solid state and can cause mechanical failure during casting. This project uses lead tracer droplets, synchrotron X-ray radiography and artificial intelligence to quantify flow between growing dendrites.

The work supports the development of impurity-tolerant aluminium alloys, helping recycled material to be used more widely and reliably in engineering applications.

I presented this work with Mehmet Emre Bozkurt and Yutong Zheng, with Shikang Feng, Professor Patrick Grant and Dr Enzo Liotti. The poster was awarded second place at the 9th International Workshop on Advances in X-ray Imaging, organised by The University of Manchester at Harwell and hosted at Diamond Light Source.
